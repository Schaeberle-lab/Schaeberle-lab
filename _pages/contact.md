## How to reach us

**By train**
Giessen Hauptbahnhof is well connected by ICE and regional trains to Frankfurt (35 min), Marburg (20 min), and Cologne (2 h). From the station, take bus line 1 or 11 to *Licher Straße / Seltersweg*.

**By car**
Giessen is located directly on the A480/A45 motorway junction. Parking is available at the IFZ campus (Interdisziplinäres Forschungszentrum), Heinrich-Buff-Ring 26.

**By plane**
The nearest airport is **Frankfurt Main (FRA)**, approximately 60 km south of Giessen (45 min by car or 1 h by direct train via Frankfurt Hauptbahnhof).
